# Portable Pinball V1.13 — Floor-anchored flipper reinforcement

Based on V1.12 without replacing it. Both front walls gain reinforced low fastening lugs, while the 4 mm front floor no longer has the V1.9–V1.12 cutouts around the old full-depth pedestals. The button hole, D40 backing nut surface, nudge switch opening, and adjustable feet remain in their prior positions.

## Fasteners

Two M3 screws (one per side) run up from the underside through 3.4 mm clearance holes at (X17,Y130) and (X243,Y130) in the assembled front floor. Each engages a standard metal M3 hex nut inserted horizontally from the rear-facing side of a low flipper wall lug. Nominal nut pocket: 6.65 mm vertex diameter, 3.0 mm tall, with a 6.25 mm side-entry slot. The nut pocket floor starts at assembled Z11. Lug bears on the floor upper surface Z4; wall/floor are **separate printable pieces**. Start fit-testing with an M3x16 machine screw, but check actual hardware length and ensure it does not bottom out. Do not tap threads into plastic.

## Checks / assembly

- First print the wall + floor fit coupons. Confirm a real nut enters the rear slot and is securely held against rotation. Check screw head and washer seating from below.
- Assemble with the nut inserted before sliding the floor into place. Align both underside screws with the wall lug and tighten gently. Do not torque hard enough to crush the 4 mm printed panel.
- Verify there is no button-hardware, switch, nudge wiring, foot mounting plate, or panel overlap in the actual cabinet.
- CAD passing clearance checks is not proof of print strength. PETG with multiple perimeters is a reasonable starting point; check the wall/rib junction under repeated flipper presses.
- All V1.12 assets and source preserved.
