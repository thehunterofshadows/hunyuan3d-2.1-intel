# Portable Pinball V1.14 — Remove extra rectangular flipper block

V1.14 keeps V1.13 as an older selectable version but removes the large rectangular extra nut-holder on **both** front sidewalls. The original continuous D-shaped reinforcement under each flipper hole remains and still rests on top of the removable 4 mm front floor. No independent mounting block/heel is left by the nudge switch.

The original lower reinforcement is gently widened inward from 8 to 11.5 mm at the foot (tapering back to original 8 mm thickness above) to retain a standard side-inserted metal M3 hex nut. This is a local, tapered change to the original rib, rather than a new protruding holder. Two underside 3.4 mm floor holes now align directly with the rib at X8 / X252 and Y86 (front), instead of the previous X17 / X243, Y130 block location. Nut is held at Z8–11 (assembled coordinates), slot width nominal 6.25 mm. Short M3 screws go upward through the intact floor to the captive nuts. Test actual screw reach; **do not reuse V1.13 16 mm screw length unverified**.

Physical print-first check: verify metal nut slide-in and rotation resistance, floor mating, washer seating, and no switch/hardware intrusion. Automated checks do not establish printed strength. Save all prior version designs.
