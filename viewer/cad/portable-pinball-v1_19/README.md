# Portable Pinball V1.19 — M5 leg-mount sandwich revision

Four receiver-to-floor assemblies use M5 nuts and M5 machine bolts; M6 modular leg connections, M8 rubber feet and the original V1.18 controls/other cabinet geometry remain unchanged. Use a test print and review hardware clearances before use.

## M5 external-hex bolt socket clearance update

All four lower `foot_receiver_*` parts have a single Ø17 mm accessible passage from the bottom of the boss to the M5 bolt head seat. The receiver flange and boss are Ø60 mm to retain >3 mm circumferential wall at the closest edge of each offset counterbore. Socket outer diameter is unmeasured; print one receiver and verify the socket fits and rotates freely. Upper backing plates, floor panels, and modular feet are unchanged.
