# Portable Pinball CAD V1.7 — tool-free rear hatch

Based on preserved V1.6; all other 43 printed parts unchanged.

- **A1 rear control deck:** original two hatch fastener X=12/104 mm, Y=opening front+8 mm now have 6.4 mm round through-holes; old M3 nut pockets are removed.
- **B1 control hatch:** removable original 100 mm-wide cover with two integral 6.0 mm diameter, 4.4 mm-long round pegs underneath, and a 10 mm diameter semicircular finger notch at its forward edge.
- **No fasteners required for the hatch:** align, lower, lift. Pins locate B1 but do not lock it for inversion or transport.
- **Fit clearance:** 0.2 mm *radial* (0.4 mm diametral); try the included `hatch_hole_6p4mm.stl` and `hatch_peg_6mm.stl` before the full A1/B1 prints. Adjust to your printer's results if loose or tight.
- **Preserved:** V1.6's strengthened splice plates, screen saddles, side walls for exciters and all other components.
